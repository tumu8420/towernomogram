TowerNomogram

Resurrecting the paper nomogram — an interactive wind-turbine tower calculator for onshore towers.

⚠ Safety disclaimer — read first. TowerNomogram is a simplified educational / preliminary-exploration tool only. It is NOT for actual structural design, certification, or construction decisions. All results are rough estimates from a simplified model and must be verified with proper engineering analysis by a qualified engineer before any real-world use.

How the calculation works

Full assumptions, formulas and limits are listed in README.md and as comments in app.js. In short:

  • Tower mass — thin-walled circular shell, linearly tapered diameter and thickness, plus an 8% allowance for flanges and internals.
  • First natural frequency — cantilever beam: f₁ = (1/2π) · √(3EIeff / H³ / meq), with meq = Mtop + 0.24 · Mtower.
  • Tip deflection — steady thrust T = ½ ρ A Ct V² at a characteristic operating wind speed, then δ = T H³ / (3EIeff).
  • Slenderness — H / Dbase.

This model ignores taper in the stiffness integral, soil–structure interaction, damping, aeroelastic effects, fatigue, buckling, transport and erection loads, and all code load cases. That is why it is an educational tool only.
